Privacy Policy

This policy describes the private, self-hosted Inkling mail integration. Each installation is operated by its owner, who is responsible for its configuration and data. The open-source repository and this static website do not receive your mailbox data.

What the assistant accesses

The assistant processes messages you send it to answer your requests. If you connect Gmail, it requests read-only mail access to find messages and read threads, and compose access to create drafts and send approved emails. This can include email addresses, headers, subjects and message contents needed for your request. Google shows the permissions before you grant access.

Connecting an account is optional. The mail-only setup requests Gmail permissions, not Calendar, Drive or other Google services. Other optional integrations require their own configuration and authorization.

How information is used and shared

Google user data is used only to provide the assistance you request. Relevant email results and content may be sent to the installation's configured AI provider to interpret your request and compose an answer or draft. Replies and confirmations travel through WhatsApp. Provider selection, processing and retention depend on the installation and that provider's applicable terms; ask the operator which provider is configured.

Mail tools are restricted to the connected person's private chat. They are not offered in group chats. Sending email requires a preview and your confirmation in a later message. Approved recipients receive the email you approve. Server administrators with access to the encryption keys can access stored information.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used by this application to train generalized AI models. It is not intentionally shared with other users. Human access is limited to your consent, necessary security or support investigations, and legal requirements.

Inkling's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth refresh tokens, chat history, conversation archives and saved notes are stored on the operator's server, separately per person and encrypted at rest. Email content included in a conversation may remain in that person's encrypted history or notes. Drafts and sent emails are also stored by Gmail in the connected account. This integration does not mirror your entire mailbox into a public repository.

Stored conversation data is retained until you reset it or request deletion; saved notes remain after a reset. The operator is responsible for the retention and deletion of any backups. Disconnecting Google does not automatically erase earlier conversations or email already saved in Gmail.

Your choices and deletion

  • Send /disconnect google in your private chat to remove the stored connection and request token revocation at Google. You can also revoke access in your Google Account settings.
  • Send /reset to clear conversation history, its archive and recap. Saved notes remain; ask the operator to delete notes or all stored data.
  • You can stop using the assistant at any time. Contact the operator in your existing private WhatsApp chat for access, correction or deletion requests, and questions about providers or backups.

This website

These static information pages have no forms, tracking scripts, analytics or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process connection information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Visiting these pages does not connect a Google account or grant mailbox access.
