Terms of Service

Inkling is open-source software used to run a private personal assistant. This website provides information; it does not provide a public hosted assistant or create an account.

Using a private installation

Use the assistant only for lawful tasks and with accounts and data you are authorized to use. Do not use it to harass, spam or access another person's accounts without permission. The operator decides who can use their installation.

Approvals and accuracy

The assistant can make mistakes. Check important information and review email recipients and contents before confirming a send. You are responsible for actions you approve. Optional integrations can change or become unavailable.

Privacy and stopping

You choose which accounts to connect. Read the Privacy Policy for how the mail integration uses data, how to disconnect and how to request deletion. Contact the operator through your existing private WhatsApp conversation with the assistant.
